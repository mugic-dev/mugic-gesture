MUGIC Gesture · Violin · bow arm

Son filé

not connected

  • Strap the MUGIC on the bow-arm forearm, just above the wrist.
  • Flick to turn ON (rising beeps).
  • Hold still for 2 s to get ready (high beep), then play.
  • Hold still again to end the take (low, then falling beeps): it turns OFF, one take per ON.
  • Or flick to turn OFF mid-take (falling beeps), which also ends the take. Either way, flicks are then ignored for at least 10 s, and until the MUGIC has been laid down still for 2 s.
  • Student takes are scored against the teacher reference. Pick Teacher only to record a reference of your own.
