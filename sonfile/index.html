MUGIC Gesture · Violin · bow arm

Son filé

not connected

  • Strap the MUGIC on the bow-arm forearm, just above the wrist.
  • Flick to turn ON (rising beeps).
  • Hold still for 2 s to get ready (high beep), then play.
  • Hold still again to end the take (low beep).
  • Flick again to turn OFF (falling beeps). Flicks are then ignored for at least 10 s, and until the MUGIC has been laid down still for 2 s.
  • Teacher takes become the reference; student takes are scored against it.
