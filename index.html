MUGIC Gesture Son filé

not connected

Strap the MUGIC on the bow-arm forearm, just above the wrist. Flick to turn ON (rising beeps), hold still for 2 s to get ready (high beep), play, hold still again to end the take (low beep). Flick again to turn OFF (falling beeps); flicks are then ignored for at least 10 s and until the MUGIC has been laid down still for 2 s. Teacher takes become the reference; student takes are scored against it.
